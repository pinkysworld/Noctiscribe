Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

1. Overview

NoctiScribe is developed by Michel Picker. This policy explains what data the app can access, how it is used, and how you can contact us.

2. Core Storage

Audio clips, transcripts, summaries, and app settings are stored on your device by default. If you enable iCloud features, selected recording data may sync through your private Apple iCloud container. HealthKit data is never included in iCloud sync.

3. Audio Recording

  • Audio recording starts only when you activate Night Mode and speech is detected.
  • Recorded clips are stored in the app's private sandbox on your device.
  • You can delete individual clips, full nights, or all stored data at any time inside the app.

4. Transcription

  • NoctiScribe offers an on-device-only speech recognition setting whenever Apple supports the required local language and language pack.
  • When you choose cloud transcription, audio is sent directly to Apple's speech recognition service.
  • The on-device-only setting never falls back to cloud processing.
  • NoctiScribe developers do not receive, store, or manually review that audio.

5. Apple Health (HealthKit)

  • If you grant permission, NoctiScribe reads Apple Health sleep and overnight vital data for on-device correlation insights.
  • NoctiScribe does not write any data back to Apple Health or include HealthKit data in iCloud sync.
  • You can revoke HealthKit permissions at any time in the Health app or in iOS Settings.

6. Optional System Services

  • Location may be used only for optional home geofence automation.
  • Calendar access may be used only for optional alarm and travel-night automation.
  • Notifications may be used for reminders, processing updates, and app alerts.

7. Analytics and Tracking

NoctiScribe does not use third-party ad SDKs or cross-app tracking. The app does not collect marketing profiles or sell personal data.

8. Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. NoctiScribe does not store your payment details.

9. Data Retention

Your data remains under your control. Removing content in the app deletes it from the local app container. If iCloud features are enabled, Apple may retain synced items in your private iCloud storage until you remove them there as well.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated over time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new update date.

11. Contact

Support and privacy questions are handled only by email: mip@gmx.biz
